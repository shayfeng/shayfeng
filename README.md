## About Me

I am an MPhil researcher in **Robotics and Autonomous Systems at HKUST (Guangzhou)**. My research focuses on **physical intelligence for generalist robot manipulation**, connecting multimodal perception, tactile sensing, closed-loop control, and robot learning.

I am particularly interested in how **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models**, **tactile feedback**, and **reinforcement learning** can help robots interact with the physical world more reliably, especially in **contact-rich and force-sensitive manipulation**.

My work emphasizes real-robot validation and the interaction between learned high-level policies and fast low-level physical feedback.

## Research Interests

- Vision-Language-Action models for robot manipulation
- Tactile and visuotactile sensing
- Contact-rich and force-sensitive manipulation
- Reinforcement learning for robotics
- Closed-loop control and reactive manipulation
- Multimodal perception and physical interaction

## Selected Publications

### TactileReflex — IROS 2026 · First Author · Accepted
**TactileReflex: Noise-Statistics-Driven Vision-Tactile Reflex Control for Force-Sensitive Manipulation**  
Ziyan Feng, Yulong Fu, Zheng Li, Yuxin He, Jieji Ren, Yudong Zhong, Lujia Wang, Jinni Zhou, Qiang Nie

A self-calibrated visuotactile reflex controller that turns intrinsic sensor-noise statistics into control thresholds, reducing reliance on external force calibration, material models, or manual tuning.

**What is the Better Curriculum: Controller-Shaped Grasping Behavior for Contact Force-Sensitive Manipulation**

Uses tactile feedback as a teacher during demonstration collection: a high-rate controller shapes grasping behavior so learned policies such as ACT and π0.5 can acquire safer contact behavior without tactile inputs at inference time.

## Research Vision

My long-term goal is to develop **general-purpose robots with robust physical intelligence**: systems that can understand open-ended human intent, perceive and reason about the real world through multiple sensing modalities, and interact with diverse objects and environments with reliability, adaptability, and safety. I am broadly interested in advancing robot learning toward agents that can generalize beyond narrowly defined tasks and acquire reusable physical capabilities that transfer across scenarios, embodiments, and environments.

### 关于我

我目前是 **香港科技大学（广州）机器人与自主系统方向 MPhil 研究生**。我的研究聚焦于面向通用机器人操作的 **Physical Intelligence（物理智能）**，重点探索多模态感知、触觉反馈、闭环控制与机器人学习之间的结合。

**TactileReflex: Noise-Statistics-Driven Vision-Tactile Reflex Control for Force-Sensitive Manipulation**

提出一种自校准视触觉反射控制方法，将触觉传感器自身的噪声统计转化为控制阈值，从而减少对外部力标定、材料模型和人工参数调节的依赖。

#### What is the Better Curriculum — CoRL 2026 · 第一作者 · Accepted
**What is the Better Curriculum: Controller-Shaped Grasping Behavior for Contact Force-Sensitive Manipulation**

利用触觉闭环控制器在数据采集阶段塑造更稳定的抓取示范，使 ACT、π0.5 等策略能够在推理阶段不输入触觉的情况下学习更可靠的接触行为。

[项目主页](https://shayfeng.github.io/better-curriculum/)
